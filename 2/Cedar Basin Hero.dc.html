The ritual

Four minutes
I
Wet the basin, not the face Two pumps of cleanser into cold standing water. Work it in the palms first so it never hits skin neat.
II
Serum while damp Three drops, pressed rather than rubbed. Wait until it stops feeling like anything at all.
III
Balm the edges, oil the rest Beard, knuckles, anywhere the weather lands first. The salve stays in a coat pocket for the same reason.

Cedarwood Fallen, never felled Steam-distilled from windfall in the same valley the basin sits in. Two barrels a year, and that sets the batch size.

The shed Nine ingredients, no lab Everything is blended, filled and labelled in one room. If a batch turns cloudy it goes to the staff shelf, not the shop.
