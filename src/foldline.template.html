Questions

The things people ask before they book.

Do I get a design, or a working page?+

A working page. I design it, you approve it, then I build the real thing in HTML and CSS — responsive, fast, and checked on a phone. You get the code and the design files either way. On Complete and Studio I also deploy it live for you.

What do you need from me to start?+

What you sell, who buys it, and any proof you already have — testimonials, numbers, screenshots, logos. A voice note works as well as a document. If you have brand colours or fonts, send them; if you don't, I'll pick and show you why.

What if I don't like the first draft?+

You point at what's wrong and I revise it — 1 round on Starter, 2 on Complete, 3 on Studio. A round covers as many comments as you want to leave at once, and they usually come back the same day. If you want more rounds after that, I quote them before I start.

Do you write the copy?+

You bring the substance — the offer, the proof, the objections you hear from buyers. On Complete and Studio I sharpen it into the headline, the section order and the CTA. You will never be handed a page full of lorem ipsum.

Is it WordPress, or a page builder?+

Neither. Pages are hand-written HTML and CSS, so there is no plugin to renew, no builder licence, and nothing to slow the page down. That means it will run anywhere — your host, Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages — and any developer can pick it up later. This site runs a WebGL scene in its hero because showing you is better than telling you. Yours won't, unless you ask for it — and if you ask, that is what Studio is for.

Who owns the work when it's done?+

You do, completely. The code, the design files and the domain are yours from the moment the project is delivered. There is nothing to keep paying me for, and nothing locked to my account.
