Questions

The things people ask before their first brief.

What does “one request at a time” mean?+

You can queue as many pages as you like, and we work through them one at a time so nothing sits half-finished. As soon as one is approved, the next starts — a typical month is three to five pages.

Do I get a design, or a working page?+

A working page. We design it, you approve it, then we build it and publish it live on your domain — forms connected, analytics wired, mobile checked. You also get the design files and the code.

What if I don't like the first draft?+

You comment and we revise — as many rounds as it takes, usually same day. Nothing is published until you press approve, and if it's still not right you can pause or cancel before the next month.

Do you write the copy?+

You bring the substance — the offer, the proof, the objections you hear on calls. We shape it into the headline, the section order and the CTA. You'll never be handed a page full of lorem ipsum.

Can agencies white-label this?+

Yes, and plenty do. We stay invisible: no branding on your deliverables, and you can invite clients into the portal under your own name.

What happens to my pages if I cancel?+

They're yours and they stay live — the domain, the files and the code were always in your name. Cancel in the dashboard whenever you like; come back when the next page is due.
